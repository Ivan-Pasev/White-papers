## Research map

| Discipline / programme | Research connection | Publication requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematical foundations | well-foundedness, invariants, recursion, MRP / OBS | explicit definitions, formal obligations, primary literature |
| Digital Fabrica Theory | typed states, transformations, trace reciprocity, continuation | semantics, proofs, executable comparators |
| Physics & field theory | FQFT, geometric and physical modeling | falsifiable predictions, dimensional consistency, empirical evidence |
| Cybernetics & systems engineering | control, stability, measurement, distributed systems | assumptions, reproducible experiments, risk and uncertainty budgets |
| AI & intelligence infrastructure | verified reasoning, provenance, model-independent systems | independent validation, adversarial tests, traceable data |

These are **research areas**, not assertions that every associated theorem or device has been validated.


### Release dossier for each paper

Each approved paper will carry a clear status (preprint / submitted / reviewed / accepted / published), full authorship and affiliations, actual citations/DOIs, claim-and-evidence appendix, data and code availability, licensing/permissions, figure provenance, limitations, a changelog and a reproducibility or verification receipt when relevant.

Scientific claims are classified against their evidence. **A Zenodo DOI is a permanent identifier, not peer review.** A formalization plan is not a proof-assistant certification. A simulation is not an experimental measurement.

## Research integrity

- No invented bibliographic references, fabricated peer review or unsupported novelty.
- No universal stability, physical discovery, quantum security or “solved problem” claim without appropriate proofs or observations.
- Negative results, open problems and counterexamples are retained.
- AI-assisted contributions and visualizations are disclosed under the target venue's rules.
- Human and organizational affiliations are reported accurately, not inferred.
- Third-party software/skills retain their attribution and license notices; author manuscripts are **not** given an implicit open license by this README.

See [Publication Policy](PUBLICATION_POLICY.md) for the acceptance gate.

## Citation

Cite the **specific published paper and its version DOI**, rather than citing this repository as if all papers formed a single reviewed article. Each release will include its own citation metadata when approved.
